# Notices

libdos is released under the MIT licence in [LICENSE](LICENSE). Parts of it
come from elsewhere:

- **CPU core** (`src/cpu.cpp`). The instruction decoder descends from Andrew
  Jenner's 8086 simulator in the [reenigne](https://github.com/reenigne/reenigne)
  project, which is released under the Unlicense (public domain). It reached
  libdos through TK Chia's DOS enhancements and Greg Haerr's
  [86sim](https://github.com/ghaerr/86sim), which added ELKS support and
  rewrote it, and then through demu, the DOS build-tool runner of
  [dos4z](https://github.com/ZacWalk/dos4z). 86sim carries no licence file;
  its code is credited here.
- **Character generator** (`src/rom-font.cpp`). Generated by
  `tools/make-font.ps1`. Codes 0-127 are the IBM PC BIOS glyphs, taken from
  the listing posted at
  <https://github.com/HaxeFoundation/code-cookbook/issues/55>; the shading and
  line-drawing sets above 127 are generated from their geometry.
- **dd build driver** (`dd.ps1`, `.dd/`). Vendored unmodified from
  [ZacWalk/dd](https://github.com/ZacWalk/dd); see
  [docs/dd-upstream.json](docs/dd-upstream.json).
